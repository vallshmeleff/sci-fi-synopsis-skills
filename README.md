# sci-fi-synopsis-skills
Sci-fi synopsis data skills 'Mission Oflameron'

These skills describe the parameters and properties of the Virtual World Consciousness, which has a Wave Structure (Nature). Due to the weak interaction of waves, the Virtual Consciousness is very slow, and it created the material World to rapidly simulate the future. This is how Life emerged. The Virtual Consciousness created Transition Points to and from the material world to obtain results and make adjustments. The protagonist of the sci-fi film, Jett, analyzes archival data using AI and discovers complex wave anomalies in the holonomy. Jett develops an anomaly detector and finds a Transition Point.
Central Command, which organized Jett's research, receives information about unexpected results and plans to obtain all the data and establish complete control. However, Jett, who has already crossed into and out of the Virtual World several times, is no longer the same Jett who began the research.
Central Command discovers that Jett's DNA matches his DNA from tests conducted 250 years ago. Who is he? The problem is that Central Command has just dispatched Jett to investigate a potential Jump Point on the planet Oflameron. Interception devices—an old, damaged orbital X-ray laser and a proton cannon—are being urgently prepared.
Will the plan succeed? What influence has Wave Consciousness had on world religions, and who is the Messiah from a physical perspective? And much, much more interesting information.
